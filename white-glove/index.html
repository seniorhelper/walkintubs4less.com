White glove walk-in tub delivery and installation

The easy button. We measure, deliver, tear out the old tub, haul it away, install the new one, test it and show you how it works. You get one written price and one crew to call.

Everything that's on a White Glove job

1. Fit check

Bathroom and doorway measurements, drain location, water heater size and the electrical panel, checked before you order so the tub you pick actually fits.

2. Delivery inside

The tub comes into the bathroom, not to the curb. Nobody in the family has to wrestle a crate up the driveway.

3. Demo and haul-away

The old tub and surround come out and leave with us, along with the job debris.

4. Set, plumb, power

The tub is leveled, plumbed and sealed. Jetted and heated models get the circuit they need from a licensed electrician.

5. Finish

Wall surround or tile flange tied in so water stays where it belongs.

6. Test and teach

A full fill, a door-seal check, a drain test and a walkthrough of every control with whoever will be bathing.

Who White Glove is for

Families buying for a parent who lives alone. Anyone recovering from surgery who can't manage a contractor. People who've been burned by a remodel before. And anyone who'd simply rather not coordinate a store, a freight company, a plumber and an electrician. White Glove costs more than Supply Only because it includes labor, materials and haul-away; your quote shows each piece so you can see exactly where the money goes.

What we won't do

We won't rush you with a "today only" price, and we won't tell you insurance or Medicare will pay for it. Original Medicare generally doesn't cover walk-in tubs. If you're a veteran or on Medicaid, there may be grant or waiver money; our sister company's VA HISA guide explains one option.

White Glove questions

How long does a white glove walk-in tub install take?

Many swaps of an old tub for a walk-in in the same spot are a one or two day job once the tub is on site. Jobs that move the drain, add a circuit, or need wall repair take longer. Your written quote lists the expected install days for your bathroom.

Do you haul away the old tub?

Yes. Removal and haul-away of the old tub and the debris from the job are part of White Glove, and they're listed on your quote so you can see them.

Is White Glove available where I live?

It depends on your ZIP code. Put it on the quote form or tell us on the phone and we'll tell you straight away whether a White Glove crew covers your area. If not, we'll price Supply Only and help you find an installer.

Will you pull the permit?

Where your city or county requires a plumbing or electrical permit for the work, it's on the quote as its own line. Requirements vary a lot from place to place, so we check yours rather than guess.

Want us to take it from here?

Give us your ZIP and a rough idea of the bathroom. We'll tell you if a crew covers your area and send one written price.
