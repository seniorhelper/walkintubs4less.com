Our white glove package covers delivery, removal, installation and haul-away, done by factory-certified installers. You pick the tub and the day. Installed pricing depends on your bathroom and the model, so your free quote itemizes every piece.

Everything on a full install

  1. Written quote first. By phone or from photos, with no high-pressure sales visit.
  2. Site visit. After you pick a tub and place a deposit, a factory-certified tech visits to measure, photograph and confirm doorways, drain side, water heater and power.
  3. Delivery inside. The tub comes into the bathroom, not to the curb.
  4. Removal and haul-away. The old tub and surround leave with the crew.
  5. Set, plumb, power. Leveled, plumbed and sealed; jetted and heated models get their circuit.
  6. Test and walkthrough. A full fill, a door-seal check, a drain test and a lesson on every control.

Curious how the tub gets through the house? Our walk-in tub delivery guide explains it step by step.

Who it is for

Families buying for a parent who lives alone. Anyone recovering from surgery. People who have been burned by a remodel. And anyone who would rather not coordinate a store, a freight company, a plumber and an electrician.

What we will not do

We will not rush you with a today-only price, and we will not tell you Medicare will pay for it: Original Medicare generally does not cover walk-in tubs. Veterans and Medicaid members may have grant or waiver options; Aging Safely Baths explains how the VA HISA grant works for bathroom changes.

Installation questions

How long does a walk-in tub install take?

1 day in most homes, once the tub is on site. Jobs that move a drain, add a circuit or need wall repair take longer, and your written quote lists the expected days.

Do you haul away the old tub?

Yes. Removal and haul-away of the old tub, the surround and the job debris are part of a full install, listed on your quote.

Is a full install available where I live?

We install in all 50 states through factory-certified installers. Put your ZIP code on the quote form or tell us by phone and we confirm coverage and timing for your area.

Will you pull the permit?

Where your city or county requires a plumbing or electrical permit, it is on the quote as its own line. Requirements vary a lot, so we check yours rather than guess.
