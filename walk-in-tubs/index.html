How to narrow it down fast

Start with the room, then the bather. Measure the alcove wall to wall and the tightest doorway on the way in; that removes most of the list. Then pick the door: inward doors suit people who can stand and step in, while outward and lift-up doors open the side of the tub for a seated transfer. Finally choose the therapy: a soaker is simplest, while water jets, air jets and heaters add comfort and a dedicated circuit.

Still between two or three? Put them in the compare tool, check the specs and rough-in table, or run your numbers through the fit planner. Weighing a big-box listing against a dealer? Read Home Depot, Lowe's and Costco walk-in tubs versus a dealer.
