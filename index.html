Quick answers

What's the difference between Supply Only and White Glove?

Supply Only means you buy the tub and we ship it; your own plumber or contractor installs it, and they can call our tech support line for help at no charge. White Glove means we do the whole job: measure, remove and haul away the old tub, set and plumb the new one, seal it and test it. Both get the same tub and the same factory warranty terms.

Can I buy a walk-in tub online here today?

Not with a card yet. Online checkout is coming. For now, tap Get my price, pick a path and a tub (or tell us you need help choosing), and you'll get a written quote that lists the tub, freight and any install work. You don't pay until you approve it.

Does Medicare pay for a walk-in tub?

Original Medicare generally does not. Walk-in tubs aren't on Medicare's durable medical equipment list. Some Medicare Advantage plans offer extra home-safety benefits, so ask your plan directly. We won't tell you a tub is covered, and you should be wary of anyone who does.
