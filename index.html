Questions buyers ask

Can I buy a walk-in tub without installation?

Yes. Choose supply only: the tub ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor installs it, with our phone tech support for them at no charge.

What does fully installed include?

A factory-certified tech does a full site visit first. On install day the old tub comes out and is hauled away, the new tub is set, plumbed, sealed and test-filled, and you get a walkthrough. Most homes are done in 1 day. Installed pricing depends on your bathroom and the model, so call for a free quote.

Which walk-in tub brand is the best value?

For most buyers, Hydro Dimensions: its published lifetime warranty covers the whole tub, motors and controls included. Rane by Bestbath is the only fully made-in-USA line we sell.

Do you ship walk-in tubs to Canada?

Yes, supply only. Freight is quoted to your postal code, and our tech line supports your installer the same way it does in the US.

Not sure which tub fits?

Call and tell us about the bathroom and the bather. We price it shipped or installed, in writing.
