Walk-In Tub Buying FAQ

Straight answers on buying, delivery, fit and installation.

Frequently asked

Can I really buy a walk-in tub without the installation?

Yes, that is supply only. You pay for the tub and freight, it is delivered to your curb or driveway, and your own installer puts it in with our phone tech support.

Why do some companies only sell walk-in tubs installed?

Many brands sell the tub and the installation as one package, so you cannot see the tub price on its own. We price the tub alone and the full job separately, so you can compare.

How wide does my doorway need to be?

Compare the narrowest doorway or hall turn with the tub's width. Many walk-ins are 26 to 33 inches wide, and the crate adds a little. If it is close, send us the measurements before you order; tubs can sometimes be tipped on edge, but not always.

Will a walk-in tub fit where my old bathtub is?

Often. Many walk-ins are built for the common 60-inch alcove, and shorter models use an extension kit or filler panel. The drain side also needs to match.

Do I need a bigger water heater?

Maybe. A walk-in holds more than a standard tub and you fill it after you sit down. Compare the tub's gallons with your heater. An inline heater keeps water warm but does not heat cold water from scratch.

How much does an installed walk-in tub cost?

Installed pricing depends on the model, therapy options and the bathroom, so call for a free quote. Supply only is the tub plus freight. Both are quoted in writing.

Does Medicare cover walk-in tubs?

Original Medicare generally does not. Some Medicare Advantage plans offer supplemental home-safety benefits, so call your plan. Medicaid waivers and VA grants are separate programs with their own rules.

Do you ship to Canada?

Yes, supply only, with freight quoted to your postal code.

Can I pay online?

Not by card. You get a written quote with the tub, freight and any installation listed, and you pay after you approve it.

What about returns?

Walk-in tubs ship by freight, so returns work differently from small parcels and vary by maker. The return and cancellation terms for your tub are on your quote before you pay.

Medicare answer based on Medicare.gov durable medical equipment rules and CMS National Coverage Determination 280.1. No bathing product eliminates the risk of falls.

Not sure which tub fits?

Call and tell us about the bathroom and the bather. We price it shipped or installed, in writing.
