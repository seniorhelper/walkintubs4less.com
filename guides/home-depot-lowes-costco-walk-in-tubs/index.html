Home Depot, Lowe's and Costco Walk-In Tubs vs Buying From a Dealer

Home Depot, Lowe's and Costco all sell walk-in tubs, and their listings are a fair place to start. On November 17, 2026 we found a Home Depot walk-in whirlpool listed at $5,926, a Lowe's tub sold "curbside delivery only," and a Costco.ca tub with a 10-year shell warranty. Here is what each actually offers, and where a dealer fits.

In short

  • Big-box stores list walk-in tubs online from several makers, with installation offered through local partners in many ZIP codes.
  • Read the delivery level on each listing. The ones we checked were curbside or did not say.
  • Warranty length varies widely by maker, from 10 years on the shell to lifetime on the whole tub.
  • A dealer sells fewer brands but usually carries every size, door and drain version, and can ship supply only or install.
  • Compare the same things for every option: model, door, warranty, delivery level and who installs.

Does Home Depot sell walk-in tubs?

Yes. Home Depot has a walk-in tub category online with filters for door side, handshower and certification. One listing we checked, the Universal Tubs Rampart 5 ft walk-in whirlpool with an adhesive wall surround, showed a price of $5,926 and a 90-day return window, in store or online. The page did not list delivery method, warranty terms or electrical requirements, so you would need to ask or open the attached documents.

Some walk-in tub listings on Home Depot are not sold as a box at all. When we loaded the category, the listings shown were tagged "Home Depot Installation Required," with a quote offered during a free design consultation. In that model, Home Depot arranges a local installer and quotes the full project. Third-party review sites describe a national network of licensed, background-checked installers, but the warranty on that work and the brands offered vary by market, so ask for both in writing.

Does Lowe's sell and install walk-in tubs?

Lowe's sells walk-in tubs online and offers installation in some areas. One listing we read, the Endurance LS Series 26 x 53 walk-in whirlpool and air tub, gives actual dimensions of 52.75 inches long, 26.375 inches wide and 37.5 inches high, and states "Curbside delivery only," meaning the carrier is not responsible for carrying it inside. Returns are "in store or ship it back for free," with eligibility rules.

For installed projects, Lowe's says it partners with Re-Bath for bathroom renovations and accessibility upgrades, and that you enter your ZIP code to see whether walk-in tubs are offered in your area. Its page says Re-Bath's workmanship, installation and products are guaranteed but does not state the length or terms on that page.

Does Costco sell walk-in tubs?

Costco.ca, the Canadian site, lists American Standard 30 x 60 walk-in tubs. The 13-jet whirlpool version we read ships by common carrier with signature required, curbside on a lift gate truck, in an estimated 20 to 30 business days. It weighs 178 pounds, has a 17-inch seat height and lists these warranty terms: 25 years on the door seal, 10 years on the tub, 5 years on components and 1 year on the faucet and hand shower.

On Costco.com in the US, we could not confirm a current walk-in tub listing on the day we checked. A third-party senior care site reports that Costco has sold Access Tubs walk-in tubs in the US, and Costco hosts an Access Tubs owner's manual, but listings come and go. If you shop Costco, check the live product page and the manual's warranty section, which for that Access model lists 10 years on the shell and frame, one year on motors and controls, and 90 days of labor.

What does a dealer do that a big-box listing usually does not?

A big-box store is built to sell many categories well. A walk-in tub dealer is built to sell one category in depth. That difference shows up in a few practical ways, none of which make one choice right for everyone.

  • Every version of a model. Walk-in tubs come in left and right door, left and right drain, soaker, air, whirlpool and combination packages. A dealer can order the exact version, while a retail site may stock only the most popular ones.
  • Brand depth. We carry the full Hydro Dimensions, Ella's Bubbles and Meditub lines, Rane transfer tubs and ANZZI walk-in tubs. You can browse them by brand starting at Hydro Dimensions, Rane or ANZZI.
  • A choice of how it gets in your house. You can buy supply only with free tech support for your contractor, or choose the white glove package with delivery, removal, install and haul-away.
  • Spec help before you buy. Drain location, door clearance and circuit needs are on our specs page, and we will read a spec sheet with you by phone.

On the installed side, our crews are factory-certified on the brands they install, which matters for warranty coverage on some lines. Aging Safely Baths explains what factory certification means for a walk-in tub install if you want the detail.

Are big-box walk-in tubs cheaper?

Sometimes the sticker is lower, and that is a real advantage for a buyer who has a trusted plumber and electrician. But a sticker price and a finished bathroom are different numbers. When you compare, line up the same items for each option:

  1. The exact model, size, door side and drain side.
  2. The therapy package: soaker, air, whirlpool or combination.
  3. What ships in the box: faucet, hand shower, extension panels, wall surround.
  4. Delivery level: curbside, threshold or room of choice.
  5. Who removes the old tub and hauls it away.
  6. Plumbing, a GFCI circuit if needed, and any water heater upgrade.
  7. Warranty by part, and whether installation by a licensed or certified installer is required to keep it.

For installed jobs, the price depends on your bathroom and the model, so we give a free quote, in writing and itemized. Supply-only prices are quoted per model because freight and package choices change the number.

Which option is right for you?

Big-box buying tends to suit people who want a specific listed model, are comfortable receiving freight at the curb, and either have their own trades or are happy with the store's local installer. A dealer tends to suit people who need a specific size or door, want help confirming the fit before buying, or want one company to handle the tub and the install. Plenty of families check both. That is a reasonable way to shop, and you can line up our tubs by size and door on the compare page or browse the full walk-in tub catalog.

Big-box vs dealer walk-in tubs: FAQ

Does Home Depot install walk-in tubs?

In many areas, yes. Some Home Depot walk-in tub listings are tagged "Home Depot Installation Required" and are quoted after a free design consultation, with the work done by a local installer. Availability, brands and installation warranty vary by market.

Does Lowe's install walk-in tubs?

Lowe's says it partners with Re-Bath for accessibility upgrades and that you can enter your ZIP code to see whether walk-in tubs are offered in your area. The tub listing we read online was sold with curbside delivery only.

Does Costco sell walk-in tubs?

Costco.ca lists American Standard 30 x 60 walk-in tubs with curbside lift gate delivery. On Costco.com in the US we could not confirm a current listing on November 17, 2026, so check the live site before you plan around it.

Is it better to buy a walk-in tub from a store or a dealer?

Neither is better for everyone. A store can be a good fit if you want a listed model and have your own trades. A dealer can be a better fit if you need a specific size, door or drain side, want spec help, or want supply-only and installed prices on the same tub.

What warranty do big-box walk-in tubs have?

It depends on the maker, not the store. The Costco.ca American Standard listing shows 25 years on the door seal, 10 on the tub, 5 on components and 1 on the faucet. Hydro Dimensions lists a lifetime warranty on the shell, frame, finish, door seal, motors and controls.
