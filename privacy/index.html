Privacy policy

Updated November 10, 2026. This policy covers walkintubs4less.com, run by Walk-In Tubs 4 Less, an Aging Safely Baths company based in Denver, Colorado.

What we collect

Only what you type into our price request form (name, phone, ZIP code, and optionally email, the tub you're interested in and notes) plus the page you sent it from, the device type (phone or desktop), any ad-campaign tags in the link you clicked, and the time. We don't collect payment details on this site.

How we use it

To answer your request: call, text or email you about the tub and path you asked about, and prepare your quote. With your consent we may contact you by phone or text, including by automated means. You can opt out any time by replying STOP to a text, telling us on a call, or emailing sales@agingsafelybaths.com.

Who sees it

Our staff and Aging Safely Baths, which handles quotes and installation. Our website and lead-handling vendors process it on our behalf. When you go ahead with a purchase, the manufacturer and freight carrier receive what they need to ship your tub. We don't sell or rent your information.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't set advertising cookies. If we add website analytics, we'll list it here and never send your name, phone or email to it.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by emailing sales@agingsafelybaths.com or calling 1-888-779-2284. Depending on your state you may have further rights; we honor them for every customer.

Security

Forms send over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our own lead server. No system is perfect, so we keep only what we need.
