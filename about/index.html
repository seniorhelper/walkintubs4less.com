Walk-In Tubs 4 Less is the online store of Aging Safely Baths, a Denver company that has installed walk-in tubs, roll-in showers and handicap showers since it was founded in 2011. Aging Safely Baths has been BBB accredited since August 2012 with an A+ rating, and its Google rating was 4.9 from 35 reviews as of October 2026.

What we do

  • Sell walk-in tubs from Hydro Dimensions, Ella's Bubbles, Meditub, Rane by Bestbath and ANZZI
  • Ship supply-only orders to all 50 states and Canada
  • Install in all 50 states through factory-certified installers, in 1 day in most homes
  • Support your own installer by phone, at no charge

How we work

Every price is itemized and in writing, by phone or from photos. There is no high-pressure sales visit. For installed jobs, a factory-certified tech does a full site visit before install day. Tubs carry their maker's warranty. Hydro Dimensions and Ella's Bubbles cover the shell for life; Meditub, Rane and ANZZI terms differ, and each model page lists them. We send the actual warranty document with every quote.
