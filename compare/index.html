What to compare first

  1. Outside size against your space. Width decides whether it gets through the door; length decides whether it fills the alcove.
  2. Door direction against the bather. Inward doors suit walkers; outward and lift-up doors suit seated transfers.
  3. Seat and step-in heights against the body. A lower step is easier on hips and knees; a seat near wheelchair height makes a transfer level.
  4. Water capacity against your heater. Bigger tubs need more hot water at once.
  5. Warranty in writing. Coverage differs a lot between makers; ask for the document, not a summary.
