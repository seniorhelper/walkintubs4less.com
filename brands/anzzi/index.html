What you get with ANZZI

ANZZI keeps the core safety features (low threshold, grab bar, slip-resistant floor, contoured seat) and keeps the price down. Soakers need no electrical work at all; the dual-therapy 30 x 53 adds 6 whirlpool jets, 18 air jets and an inline heater, and needs a circuit.

The curbside catch

ANZZI's carrier unloads the tub at the curb and leaves. That is fine with two strong helpers and an installer lined up. If not, choose a full install and we bring it in, set it and haul the old tub away.

Extension kits explained

Short ANZZI models ship with matching extension kits (7 inches on the 53-inch tubs, 14 inches on the 26 x 46) so the tub fills a standard 60-inch alcove.

Buying from a store or a dealer? Compare the options in our big-box versus dealer guide, and check whether the tub fits through your doors before delivery day.

Questions buyers ask

Are ANZZI walk-in tubs good?

They are a value line: the core safety features and a lifetime-warranted door seal at a lower price than most makers. The trade-off is more of the install on you, which is where our tech support helps.

Do ANZZI walk-in tubs fit a 60-inch bathtub space?

The 30 x 60 fits directly. The 53-inch models use a 7-inch extension kit, and the 26 x 46 uses a 14-inch kit, to fill a 60-inch alcove.

Not sure which tub fits?

Call and tell us about the bathroom and the bather. We price it shipped or installed, in writing.
