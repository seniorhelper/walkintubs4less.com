ANZZI walk-in tubs

ANZZI is a bath brand known for lower-priced walk-in tubs that still include a quick fill faucet, a handheld shower and a lifetime-warranted door seal. Its walk-in lineup runs from plain soakers to tubs with whirlpool jets, air jets and an inline heater.

The ANZZI walk-in lines

  • Value Series: 26 x 53 and 30 x 60 sizes, soaking and air versions. The simplest, lowest-maintenance option.
  • Coupe Premier: 28 inches wide, 53 inches long, with a 7-inch extension kit to fill a 60-inch alcove. Soaking, whirlpool and air-plus-whirlpool versions, in white or biscuit.
  • Fully loaded models: whirlpool plus air jets, inline maintenance heater, waterfall filler and handheld shower, including a wheelchair-access version.

What every ANZZI walk-in shares

Per ANZZI's own listings: marine-grade acrylic with fiberglass reinforcement on a freestanding steel frame, a low entry threshold, a built-in grab bar, a slip-resistant textured floor, contoured seating and a door seal covered by a lifetime warranty.

The catch with buying ANZZI online

ANZZI ships walk-in tubs curbside only: the driver unloads at the truck and leaves. That's fine if you have help and an installer lined up, and it's exactly why our Supply Only path includes tech support. If you'd rather not deal with any of it, White Glove brings the tub inside and installs it.

ANZZI questions

Are ANZZI walk-in tubs good?

They're a value brand: you get the core safety features (low threshold, grab bar, textured floor, lifetime door seal) at a lower price than most premium makers. The trade-off is that you're more on your own for installation, which is where a dealer with tech support matters.

Can ANZZI tubs fit a standard 60-inch bathtub space?

Yes. The 30 x 60 models fit directly, and the 53-inch Coupe Premier models include a 7-inch extension kit for a 60-inch alcove.
