Rane walk-in tubs

Rane Bathing Systems builds walk-in tubs in Sparta, Tennessee, where it has manufactured since 1997, and calls its tubs 100% made in the USA. Rane makes residential tubs and professional healthcare tubs, and its healthcare engineering (thermostatic mixing valves, disinfection systems, high weight ratings) carries into the home models.

Two Rane models we can tell you about in detail

  • RK12 Winnipeg: an outward-swinging-door transfer tub rated for a 600 lb occupant, 48 gallons at overflow, with a built-in disinfectant system and factory-installed air spa. Full RK12 specs.
  • RR7 Atlantic: a transition/transfer tub with an upward-swinging door, a contoured reclining seat, a recline feature to cut the chill while it fills, battery backup and a built-in disinfectant system. Designed for transfers from a wheelchair, walker or lift.

Who should look at Rane

Rane is worth a look when the bather is a larger adult, uses a wheelchair or lift, or shares the tub with others in the home (the disinfecting cycle is a real plus there). Rane's residential range also includes inward and outward door styles, compact sizes and bariatric options.

Built to healthcare codes

Rane's spec sheets list compliance with CSA B45.5 / ANSI Z124 for plastic plumbing fixtures, ASME A112.19.7M for whirlpool bathtub appliances, ASME A112.19.8M for suction fittings and UL 1795 for hydromassage bathtubs.

Rane questions

Where are Rane walk-in tubs made?

In Sparta, Tennessee. Rane says it has manufactured there since 1997 and describes its tubs as 100% made in the USA.

Are Rane tubs good for wheelchair users?

Several Rane models are built for transfers. The RK12 Winnipeg and RR7 Atlantic both list seat heights designed for transfer from a wheelchair or walker, and the Atlantic is designed for lifts too.

Is a Rane transfer tub right for your bather?

Tell us how the bather gets in today (standing, walker, wheelchair or lift) and we'll match a Rane model and door style.
