Who should look at Rane

Rane makes sense when the bather transfers from a wheelchair or a lift, when more than one person in the home uses the tub, or when a caregiver runs the bath. The RK12 Winnipeg uses an outward door and a 600 lb occupant rating; the RR7 Atlantic uses a lift-up door and a reclining seat. Both are heavy, engineered pieces, so most homes choose a full install.

Built to plumbing and hydromassage standards

Rane's sheets list CSA B45.5 / ANSI Z124 for plastic plumbing fixtures, ASME A112.19.7M for whirlpool bathtub appliances, ASME A112.19.8M for suction fittings and UL 1795 for hydromassage bathtubs.

More Rane models

Rane also builds residential tubs beyond the two we list in detail. Ask us for a specific Rane model by name and we confirm its current specs with Bestbath before quoting.

Buying from a store or a dealer? Compare the options in our big-box versus dealer guide, and check whether the tub fits through your doors before delivery day.

Questions buyers ask

Where are Rane walk-in tubs made?

In Sparta, Tennessee, by Rane Bathing Systems, part of Bestbath. It is the only line we sell that is fully made in the USA.

Are Rane tubs good for wheelchair users?

Yes. The RK12 Winnipeg uses an outward-swinging door and the RR7 Atlantic a lift-up door, both designed for seated transfers from a wheelchair, walker or lift.

Not sure which tub fits?

Call and tell us about the bathroom and the bather. We price it shipped or installed, in writing.
