Why buyers pick Meditub

When the space is unusual, Meditub often has the size. Short front-entry models such as the 2739, 3140 and 3238 fit rooms where a long tub never could, while the 3660 gives a 36-inch-wide bathing well for larger bathers. Shells are acrylic, and fiberglass versions are also made.

WCA models for wheelchair users

The WCA suffix marks Meditub's wheelchair-access tubs. The 2953WCA and 3060WCA use outward doors so the bather can transfer across from a chair.

Buying Meditub supply only

Soaker builds are simple to set; air, hydro and dual therapy builds need a dedicated circuit. We confirm the drain side and door hand on your quote.

Questions buyers ask

What do Meditub model numbers mean?

The digits are the approximate width and length in inches, so a 3060 is about 30 by 60. The exact outside size is on each model page.

Does Meditub make a wheelchair-accessible walk-in tub?

Yes. Models ending in WCA, such as the 2953WCA and 3060WCA, are wheelchair-access versions with outward-swinging doors.
