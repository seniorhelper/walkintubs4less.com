MediTub walk-in tubs

MediTub sorts its walk-in tubs into four therapy lines: Soaker, Air Therapy, Hydro Therapy and Dual Therapy. Its newest additions are wheelchair-accessible walk-in tubs.

The four MediTub lines

  • Soaker: deep water, no jets, nothing to wire for massage.
  • Air Therapy: air bubbles for a gentle all-over massage.
  • Hydro Therapy: water jets for targeted massage.
  • Dual Therapy: both air and water jets.

Sizes range from compact tubs for small bathrooms up to large two-person-width models; the model number usually encodes the width and length (for example, a 3060 is about 30 by 60 inches). Wheelchair-accessible versions carry a WCA suffix.

Buying MediTub from us

MediTub itself offers full installation only on select products. Through us, any MediTub we carry can go either way: shipped Supply Only with tech support for your installer, or White Glove installed where we have a crew.

Two ways to buy a MediTub tub

MediTub soakers are a natural Supply Only pick. The air, hydro and dual lines need power for pumps and blowers, so many buyers of those choose White Glove, or book an electrician before the tub arrives.

MediTub questions

What do MediTub model numbers mean?

In most cases the digits are the tub's approximate width and length in inches, so a 3060 is about 30 x 60 and a 2653 is about 26 x 53. Always check the spec sheet for exact exterior dimensions.

Does MediTub make a wheelchair-accessible walk-in tub?

Yes. MediTub lists wheelchair-accessible walk-in tubs as its newest models; they use a WCA suffix in the model number.

Give us the alcove width and length and we'll show you which MediTub model numbers fit, with both prices.
