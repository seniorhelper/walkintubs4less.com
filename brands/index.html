Which brand is the best value?

For most buyers, Hydro Dimensions. Its published lifetime warranty covers the whole tub, motors and controls included, and its sizes fit the bathrooms most people have. If made in the USA matters most, Rane by Bestbath is the only fully USA-made line we sell. If the bather needs a transfer door, two seats or a lay-down tub, Ella's Bubbles has the widest choice. If the budget is tight and you have a capable installer, ANZZI is the lowest-cost route.
