Walk-in tub brands we sell

Five makers under one roof, so the advice is about your bathroom, not about clearing one brand's inventory. Every brand can be bought Supply Only or White Glove.

  • ANZZIANZZI is a bath brand known for lower-priced walk-in tubs that still include a quick fill faucet, a handheld shower and a lifetime-warranted door seal.
  • RaneRane Bathing Systems builds walk-in tubs in Sparta, Tennessee, where it has manufactured since 1997, and calls its tubs 100% made in the USA.
  • Hydro DimensionsHydro Dimensions has built walk-in tubs for more than 20 years and says its tubs are built in the USA.
  • MediTubMediTub sorts its walk-in tubs into four therapy lines: Soaker, Air Therapy, Hydro Therapy and Dual Therapy.
  • Ella's BubblesElla's Bubbles is a Chicago walk-in tub company with one of the widest lineups in the industry, from petite tubs to two-seat and lay-down models.

How to choose between brands

Start with the person, not the logo. A wheelchair user needs a transfer-height seat and a door that opens wide on the side they transfer from; Rane and Ella's Bubbles build many of those. A tight budget and a standard 60-inch alcove point to ANZZI's Value Series or a MediTub soaker. Someone who wants massage should pick a therapy system first (Hydro Dimensions organizes its whole line that way) and then make sure the house can power it.

Then check the house: doorway widths on the way in, the alcove size, which side the drain is on, and how many gallons the water heater makes. We'll do that check with you for free before you order.

Not sure which path fits?

Call and tell us about the bathroom and who's using it. We'll price both paths so you can compare them side by side.
