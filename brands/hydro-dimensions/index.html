Hydro Dimensions walk-in tubs

Hydro Dimensions has built walk-in tubs for more than 20 years and says its tubs are built in the USA. Instead of naming lots of model lines, it organizes tubs by therapy system, so the first choice is how you want the water to feel.

The seven Hydro Dimensions therapy systems

  • Deep Soak: no jets, just depth.
  • Hydro Massage and Air Massage: water jets or air bubbles.
  • Dual Massage, Dual Jetted Massage and Dual Jetted Air Massage: combinations of the two.
  • PureBubbles Skin Therapy: Hydro Dimensions' fine-bubble system.

Sizes run from compact to large deep-soak models. As one spec-sheet example, the Hydro Dimensions model 2851 lists a 75-gallon operating capacity and an optional factory-installed tile flange kit.

Buying Hydro Dimensions from us

Pick a size and a therapy system and we'll price it both ways. For Supply Only, your installer gets the factory schematic for the exact model and our tech line. Comparing every Hydro Dimensions model side by side? Our sister company keeps a full model comparison.

Two ways to buy a Hydro Dimensions tub

Deep Soak models are the easiest Supply Only installs because there's nothing to wire for jets. For the dual-massage systems, either have a licensed electrician lined up or choose White Glove and we coordinate the electrical.

Hydro Dimensions questions

Are Hydro Dimensions tubs made in the USA?

Hydro Dimensions says its tubs are built in the USA. Ask us for the spec sheet of the exact model you're considering.

Which Hydro Dimensions therapy system should I pick?

If you mainly want an easy, safe bath, Deep Soak is simplest and needs no jet circuit. Choose air or water jets for massage, and the dual systems if you want both. Each jet system adds electrical work, which matters for Supply Only installs.

Picking a Hydro Dimensions therapy system?

Tell us what you want from the bath and the size of the space, and we'll price the matching model shipped or installed.
