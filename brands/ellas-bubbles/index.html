When Ella's Bubbles is the right call

If the bather uses a wheelchair, is tall, is a larger adult, or wants to lie back rather than sit upright, Ella usually has a shape that fits. The Transfer series lines its L-shaped door up with the seat for a sideways move from a chair. The Big4One, ShaK and Big4Two are built for bigger and taller bathers, and the Lay Down tubs let you recline.

Reading Ella's published fit data

Ella publishes seat width, seat depth, maximum seated hip width and a bather height range for most models. Those four numbers are the best way to match a tub to a body, and they are on each model page here.

Buying Ella supply only

Larger Ella tubs need a wider route into the bathroom and, for outswing doors, clear floor in front of the tub. We confirm both before anything ships.

Want every Ella's Bubbles model compared on one page, with installation notes? Aging Safely Baths keeps a full Ella's Bubbles model comparison.

Buying from a store or a dealer? Compare the options in our big-box versus dealer guide, and check whether the tub fits through your doors before delivery day.

Questions buyers ask

What does the Ella's Bubbles warranty cover?

Ella's warranty document lists lifetime coverage on the shell, frame and door gasket and 5 years on hardware and components. Ella does not pay labor, so the installer's own labor warranty matters; we explain ours on your quote.

Which Ella tub is best for a wheelchair user?

Start with the Transfer series (Transfer26, Transfer30, Transfer32, Transfer60 and TransferXXL). Their L-shaped outward doors open the side of the tub at seat level. Tell us the bather's side of transfer and we order the right door hand.

Not sure which tub fits?

Call and tell us about the bathroom and the bather. We price it shipped or installed, in writing.
