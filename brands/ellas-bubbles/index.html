Ella's Bubbles walk-in tubs

Ella's Bubbles is a Chicago walk-in tub company with one of the widest lineups in the industry, from petite tubs to two-seat and lay-down models. It's especially known for wheelchair-transfer tubs with wide, L-shaped outward doors.

Why people pick Ella's Bubbles

  • Transfer doors. Wide outward-opening and L-shaped doors that make sliding over from a wheelchair easier.
  • Lots of sizes. Compact tubs for small bathrooms through two-seat, extra-wide and lay-down models.
  • Therapy options. Soaking, air, hydro massage and combinations, plus extras such as heated seats and fast-drain systems on many models.

Buying Ella's Bubbles from us

Ella's Bubbles sells through dealers. With us you choose: the tub ships Supply Only to your address with our tech support for your installer, or we bring it in and install it White Glove. Tell us the model name, or tell us about the bather and the bathroom and we'll narrow it down. Want to see every Ella model side by side first? Our sister company keeps an Ella model guide.

Two ways to buy a Ella's Bubbles tub

Ella's larger transfer and two-seat tubs often need a wider path into the bathroom and floor space for the door, so we usually start with a measurement call. After that it's your choice: shipped to your installer, or brought in and installed by us.

Ella's Bubbles questions

Where is Ella's Bubbles based?

Chicago, Illinois. Industry listings give the company's address on South Carpenter Street in Chicago.

Can I buy an Ella's Bubbles tub without installation?

Yes, through our Supply Only path. The tub ships to you, and your plumber or contractor installs it with our tech support available by phone.

Shopping for an Ella transfer or two-seat tub?

Tell us about the bather and the room. We'll narrow Ella's big lineup to two or three models and price them.
