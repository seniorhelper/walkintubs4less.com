Meditub 2653 at a glance

The Meditub 2653 is a 26 by 53 inch walk-in tub from Meditub, Walk-In Series. At 53 inches long it sits short of a 60-inch alcove, so the gap is closed with a filler panel or a short wall build-out, and your quote names which one. The door opens into the tub, so the water's weight helps press it against the gasket. The bather steps in past the open door, which works best for people who can stand and walk in on their own or with a cane.

The Meditub 2653 is a 26 x 53 inch walk-in that stands 38 inches tall. Its short footprint is aimed at small bathrooms and corner layouts. Meditub backs the shell for 10 years and the door seal for life, per its own manual.

2653 questions

Will the Meditub 2653 fit through my doorways?

The shell is 26 inches wide and 38 inches tall. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 28 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

Can I buy the Meditub 2653 supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. The soaker version needs no power; jetted, air or heated versions need a dedicated circuit, so line up an electrician if you choose one.

What warranty comes with the Meditub 2653?

10 years on shell, frame and finish; 1 year on motors and controls; lifetime on the door seal; 90 days labor (register within 90 days). We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
