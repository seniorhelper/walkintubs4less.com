Hydro Dimensions 3260D at a glance

The Hydro Dimensions 3260D is a 32 by 60 inch walk-in tub from Hydro Dimensions, HD Specialty Series. At 60 inches long it is sized for the common 60-inch alcove that a standard bathtub leaves behind. The door opens into the tub, so the water's weight helps press it against the gasket. The bather steps in past the open door, which works best for people who can stand and walk in on their own or with a cane.

The Hydro Dimensions 3260D belongs to the HD Specialty Series and is built to order in Temecula, California, at 32 by 60 inches and 46 inches tall. The factory drawing shows a 6-inch step-in, a seat 17 inches off the floor and a 90-gallon operating capacity. Its extra-tall wall lets water rise higher on a seated bather than a standard-height model.

3260D questions

Will the Hydro Dimensions 3260D fit through my doorways?

The shell is 32 inches wide and 46 inches tall. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 34 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

How much hot water does the Hydro Dimensions 3260D use?

Plan on about 90 gallons. You fill a walk-in after you sit down and close the door, so the water heater has to deliver most of that as hot water in one go. Tell us your heater size and we flag it on your quote if it looks short.

Can I buy the Hydro Dimensions 3260D supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. The soaker version needs no power; jetted, air or heated versions need a dedicated circuit, so line up an electrician if you choose one.

What warranty comes with the Hydro Dimensions 3260D?

Lifetime warranty on shell, frame, finish, door seal, motors and controls. We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
