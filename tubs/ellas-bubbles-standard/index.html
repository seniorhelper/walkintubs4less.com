Ella's Bubbles Standard at a glance

The Ella's Bubbles Standard is a 30 by 52 inch walk-in tub from Ella's Bubbles, Ella Acrylic Inswing Door. At 52 inches long it sits short of a 60-inch alcove, so the gap is closed with a filler panel or a short wall build-out, and your quote names which one. The door opens into the tub, so the water's weight helps press it against the gasket. The bather steps in past the open door, which works best for people who can stand and walk in on their own or with a cane.

Ella's Bubbles lists the Standard in its Ella Acrylic Inswing Door group, sized at 30 by 52 inches. Its seat is 22 inches wide and the listed bather height range is 4'11" to 6'1". The door swings into the tub, so the bather sits down before it is latched and the tub is filled.

Standard questions

Will the Ella's Bubbles Standard fit through my doorways?

The shell is 30 inches wide. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 32 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

Can I buy the Ella's Bubbles Standard supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. The soaker version needs no power; jetted, air or heated versions need a dedicated circuit, so line up an electrician if you choose one.

What warranty comes with the Ella's Bubbles Standard?

Lifetime on shell, frame and door gasket; 5 years on hardware and components; labor not covered by Ella. We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
