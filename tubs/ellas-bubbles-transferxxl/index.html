Ella's Bubbles TransferXXL at a glance

The Ella's Bubbles TransferXXL is a 36 by 55 inch walk-in tub from Ella's Bubbles, L-Shape Wheelchair Transfer Door. At 55 inches long it sits short of a 60-inch alcove, so the gap is closed with a filler panel or a short wall build-out, and your quote names which one. The door opens out into the room, which leaves the whole opening clear for a sideways slide from a wheelchair or a walker seat. Plan for open floor in front of the tub so the door can swing fully.

Ella's Bubbles lists the TransferXXL in its L-Shape Wheelchair Transfer Door group, sized at 36 by 55 inches. Its seat is 29 inches wide and the listed bather height range is 5'1" to 6'4". Its outward door opens across the seat area, so a bather can slide over from a wheelchair rather than step over a wall.

TransferXXL questions

Will the Ella's Bubbles TransferXXL fit through my doorways?

The shell is 36 inches wide. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 38 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

Can a wheelchair user get into the Ella's Bubbles TransferXXL?

Yes, that is what it is built for. The outward swing door opens the side of the tub so the bather slides across from the chair to the seat instead of stepping over a wall. Tell us which side the bather transfers from so we order the right hand.

Can I buy the Ella's Bubbles TransferXXL supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. The soaker version needs no power; jetted, air or heated versions need a dedicated circuit, so line up an electrician if you choose one.

What warranty comes with the Ella's Bubbles TransferXXL?

Lifetime on shell, frame and door gasket; 5 years on hardware and components; labor not covered by Ella. We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
