Rane RR7-II Atlantic at a glance

The Rane RR7-II Atlantic is a 34 by 73 inch walk-in tub from Rane by Bestbath, Reclining transfer tub (Professional Health Care Series). At 73 inches long it needs more room than a standard 60-inch tub space, so it suits a larger bathroom, an open wall or a full remodel. The door lifts up instead of swinging, so the side of the tub opens for a seated transfer from a wheelchair or a patient lift.

The Rane RR7 Atlantic is built in Sparta, Tennessee and measures 34 by 73 inches. It is a transfer-style tub engineered from Rane's healthcare line for bathers who cannot step over a wall.

RR7-II Atlantic questions

Will the Rane RR7-II Atlantic fit through my doorways?

The shell is 34 inches wide and 51 inches tall. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 36 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

Can a wheelchair user get into the Rane RR7-II Atlantic?

Yes, that is what it is built for. The lift-up door door opens the side of the tub so the bather slides across from the chair to the seat instead of stepping over a wall. The seat sits 21 inches off the floor. Tell us which side the bather transfers from so we order the right hand.

Can I buy the Rane RR7-II Atlantic supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. Because it runs jets, air or a heater, line up a licensed electrician for its dedicated circuit before delivery day.

What warranty comes with the Rane RR7-II Atlantic?

5 years on shell and external finishes; 3 years on parts; lifetime on the door seal with preventive maintenance. We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
