What your installer needs to know

Because this is a soaker, there's no pump and no jet circuit to run. Your installer's main jobs are the drain hookup on the correct side, leveling the frame, tying the tile flange into the wall and running the door-seal test before the walls close up. The bigger planning question is hot water: a 60-inch walk-in holds a lot more than a standard tub and you fill it after you're seated, so check the water heater against the gallon figure on ANZZI's spec sheet before you order.
