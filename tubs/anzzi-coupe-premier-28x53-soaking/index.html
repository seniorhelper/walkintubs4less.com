What your installer needs to know

The 53-inch body plus the 7-inch extension panel is how this tub drops into a normal 60-inch tub alcove. Have your installer dry-fit the extension before setting the tub, and remember there's no tile flange: plan the wall surround or tile detail so water can't get behind it. As with any walk-in, size the hot water to the tub's capacity on the spec sheet.
