Ella's Bubbles Escape at a glance

The Ella's Bubbles Escape is a 36 by 72 inch walk-in tub from Ella's Bubbles, Two Seat Inswing Door. At 72 inches long it needs more room than a standard 60-inch tub space, so it suits a larger bathroom, an open wall or a full remodel. The door opens into the tub, so the water's weight helps press it against the gasket. The bather steps in past the open door, which works best for people who can stand and walk in on their own or with a cane.

Ella's Bubbles lists the Escape in its Two Seat Inswing Door group, sized at 36 by 72 inches. Its seat is 29 inches wide and the listed bather height range is 5'1" to 6'5". Two seats face each other around a centered door, so two people can bathe at once.

Escape questions

Will the Ella's Bubbles Escape fit through my doorways?

The shell is 36 inches wide. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 38 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

Do two people fit in the Ella's Bubbles Escape?

It has two seats in a 36 by 72 inch shell, so two adults can bathe side by side. Many buyers also use the second seat as room for a caregiver to reach in. Check the hot water: a two-seat tub holds far more water than a standard bathtub.

Can I buy the Ella's Bubbles Escape supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. Because it runs jets, air or a heater, line up a licensed electrician for its dedicated circuit before delivery day.

What warranty comes with the Ella's Bubbles Escape?

Lifetime on shell, frame and door gasket; 5 years on hardware and components; labor not covered by Ella. We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
