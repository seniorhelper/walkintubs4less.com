ANZZI Coupe Series 27 x 39 at a glance

The ANZZI Coupe Series 27 x 39 is a 27 by 39 inch walk-in tub from ANZZI. At 39 inches long it is meant for small bathrooms and old shower spaces rather than a full tub alcove. The wide door closes on a pressure seal, and the door must be latched before the tub is filled.

Coupe Series 27 x 39 questions

Will the ANZZI Coupe Series 27 x 39 fit through my doorways?

The shell is 27 inches wide. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 29 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

Can I buy the ANZZI Coupe Series 27 x 39 supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway (ANZZI ships curbside only) and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. The soaker version needs no power; jetted, air or heated versions need a dedicated circuit, so line up an electrician if you choose one.

What warranty comes with the ANZZI Coupe Series 27 x 39?

Lifetime warranty on the door pressure seal; full terms in ANZZI's warranty document. We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
