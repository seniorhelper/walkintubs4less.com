A good fit for

  • Wheelchair or walker users who transfer onto the seat
  • Larger bathers (600 lb occupant rating)
  • Households that want a built-in disinfecting cycle between users

Probably not for

  • Very tight bathrooms where an outward door can't swing open
  • Buyers who want a plain soaker at the lowest price

What your installer needs to know

Two numbers matter most to your installer: the stud opening (nominal size plus a quarter inch, never smaller) and the roughly 905-pound loaded weight, which your contractor should check against the floor framing. The outward door needs clear floor space in front of it, and the air spa and disinfectant system need power, so plan the electrical from the spec sheet. Lifts: Rane says most floor and ceiling lifts are compatible.
