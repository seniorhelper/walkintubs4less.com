Rane RK12 Winnipeg at a glance

The Rane RK12 Winnipeg is a 33.25 by 61.5 inch walk-in tub from Rane by Bestbath, Transfer tub (home and healthcare). At 61.5 inches long it is sized for the common 60-inch alcove that a standard bathtub leaves behind. The door opens out into the room, which leaves the whole opening clear for a sideways slide from a wheelchair or a walker seat. Plan for open floor in front of the tub so the door can swing fully.

The Rane RK12 Winnipeg is built in Sparta, Tennessee and measures 33.25 by 61.5 inches. It is a transfer-style tub engineered from Rane's healthcare line for bathers who cannot step over a wall. Listed capacity is 48 gallons.

RK12 Winnipeg questions

Will the Rane RK12 Winnipeg fit through my doorways?

The shell is 33.25 inches wide. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 35.25 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

Can a wheelchair user get into the Rane RK12 Winnipeg?

Yes, that is what it is built for. The outward swing door opens the side of the tub so the bather slides across from the chair to the seat instead of stepping over a wall. Tell us which side the bather transfers from so we order the right hand.

Can I buy the Rane RK12 Winnipeg supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. Because it runs jets, air or a heater, line up a licensed electrician for its dedicated circuit before delivery day.

What warranty comes with the Rane RK12 Winnipeg?

5 years on shell and external finishes; 3 years on parts; lifetime on the door seal with preventive maintenance. We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
