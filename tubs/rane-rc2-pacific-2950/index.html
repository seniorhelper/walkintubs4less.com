Rane RC2 Pacific at a glance

The Rane RC2 Pacific is a 29 by 50 inch walk-in tub from Rane by Bestbath, Residential. At 50 inches long it sits short of a 60-inch alcove, so the gap is closed with a filler panel or a short wall build-out, and your quote names which one. The door opens into the tub, so the water's weight helps press it against the gasket. The bather steps in past the open door, which works best for people who can stand and walk in on their own or with a cane.

The Rane RC2 Pacific is built in Sparta, Tennessee and measures 29 by 50 inches. It has an inward-opening door that can be hinged on either side. Listed capacity is 62 gallons.

RC2 Pacific questions

Will the Rane RC2 Pacific fit through my doorways?

The shell is 29 inches wide and 41 inches tall. Measure the narrowest door, hall turn and stair landing between the street and the bathroom. You want roughly 31 inches clear at the tightest point, or enough room to tip the crate on edge. Send us the numbers and we confirm the route before anything ships.

How much hot water does the Rane RC2 Pacific use?

Plan on about 62 gallons. You fill a walk-in after you sit down and close the door, so the water heater has to deliver most of that as hot water in one go. Tell us your heater size and we flag it on your quote if it looks short.

Can I buy the Rane RC2 Pacific supply only?

Yes. It ships by freight to your curb or driveway and your own plumber or contractor sets it, with our phone tech support behind them. The soaker version needs no power; jetted, air or heated versions need a dedicated circuit, so line up an electrician if you choose one.

What warranty comes with the Rane RC2 Pacific?

5 years on shell and external finishes; 3 years on parts; lifetime on the door seal with preventive maintenance. We send the maker's warranty document with your written quote so you can read the exact terms, including what it expects from the installation.
